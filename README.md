# GenomIA

### Exploring Chilean genomes through bioinformatics and artificial intelligence

**[GenomIA](https://genomia.cl/)** is a research and development initiative at the [**Universidad de O’Higgins, Chile**](https://www.uoh.cl/), bringing together genomics, bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence to help people explore and understand their genetic information.

We are developing a platform that connects whole-genome sequencing with accessible explanations, while advancing research into the genetic diversity of the Chilean population.

## Our mission

Make genomic information easier to understand and contribute tools and knowledge that better represent Chilean populations in genomic research.

## What we are building

- **Genomic analysis workflows** for short- and long-read sequencing, including single-nucleotide variants, insertions and deletions, and structural variants.
- **Ancestry resources** to study population history and genetic diversity in Chile.
- **Variant interpretation tools** that integrate annotations, population frequencies, and scientific evidence.
- **Pharmacogenomics and genetic risk modules** to explore how genetic variation relates to medication response and health-related traits.
- **Epigenomic analyses** using DNA methylation information from nanopore sequencing.
- **AI-assisted exploration** to help users navigate genomic results and understand the evidence and limitations behind them.

## This GitHub organization

This organization brings together the software, analysis workflows, documentation, and educational resources developed for GenomIA. Individual repositories describe their purpose, development status, installation steps, and terms of use.

Our work draws on the expertise of [**DiGenoma Lab**](https://digenoma-lab.netlify.app/), [**seqUOH**](https://sequoh.cl/), and the [**Kütral high-performance computing infrastructure**](https://www.hpc-uoh.cl/) at [Universidad de O’Higgins](https://www.uoh.cl/).
