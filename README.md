# GenomIA

### Exploring Chilean genomes through bioinformatics and artificial intelligence

**[GenomIA](https://genomia.cl/)** is a research and development initiative at the [**Universidad de O’Higgins, Chile**](https://www.uoh.cl/), bringing together genomics, bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence to help people explore and understand their genetic information.

We are developing a platform that connects whole-genome sequencing with accessible explanations, while advancing research into the genetic diversity of the Chilean population.
